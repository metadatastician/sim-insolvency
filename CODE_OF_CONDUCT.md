# Code of Conduct

Participation must be respectful, accessible and evidence-minded. Harassment,
discrimination, doxxing, learner shaming, misuse of fictional scenarios as
legal advice, and misrepresentation of assessment status are unacceptable.
Maintainers may remove harmful content or participation and will preserve
confidentiality where safety permits.
