= Governance

The `metadatastician` maintainers steward code and authored learning material.
Legal-rule activation additionally requires the two-person review defined in
`docs/rules/review-policy.adoc`. Credential/accreditation claims require a
separate external agreement and governance decision. ADRs are append-only;
supersession records a new ADR rather than rewriting history.

== Solo-maintainer reality

Programme capacity is currently concentrated in a single maintainer. This is
a registered programme risk, `KEY-004` in `programme/key-issues.toml`, gated
at `G6`. There is no quorum: no decision recorded here or in an ADR requires
more than one maintainer's sign-off unless a policy (such as the legal-rule
two-person review above) says otherwise. Contributors and agents open pull
requests; per `CONTRIBUTING.adoc`, only the owner merges.

== Decision records

Architectural decisions are recorded as ADRs under `docs/architecture/adr/`
(append-only, superseded rather than rewritten). Programme-level decisions,
risk exposure and gate status are tracked in the programme registers under
`programme/` (`key-issues.toml`, `gates.toml`, `lifecycle.toml`,
`event-register.toml`, `wiki-page-register.toml`). Both are the source of
record; narrative documentation elsewhere should point back to them rather
than restate their content.
