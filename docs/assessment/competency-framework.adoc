= Competency Framework
:toc: left

[cols="2,4",options="header"]
|===
|Competency |Observable evidence

|Engagement and independence |Conflict enquiry, scope, authority and escalation.
|Information gathering |Proportionate requests, source testing and follow-up.
|Financial analysis |Cash, solvency, asset, liability and scenario analysis.
|Issue recognition |Material issues identified without encoding conclusions.
|Procedure comparison |Eligibility, purpose, costs, control, time and outcomes.
|Legal/procedural reasoning |Use of compatible reviewed authority and caveats.
|Evidence use |Claims linked to disclosed sources; contradictions addressed.
|Uncertainty handling |Unknowns stated, impact calibrated and resolution planned.
|Case strategy |Coherent sequence under time/resource constraints.
|Prioritisation |Protection and deadline work precede lower-value enquiry.
|Timeliness |Action taken while options/evidence remain available.
|Proportionality |Cost and depth matched to risk and likely benefit.
|Asset protection |Control, preservation and realisation risks considered.
|Investigation |Potential misconduct explored without premature conclusion.
|Stakeholder treatment |Employees, secured/unsecured creditors and directors
treated consistently with role and evidence.
|Communication |Clear, timely, audience-appropriate and uncertainty-honest.
|Record quality |Decision, alternatives, evidence, authority and dissent recorded.
|Ethical reasoning |Conflicts, independence, candour and boundaries handled.
|Escalation |Professional review/referral sought when authority is insufficient.
|Closure discipline |Outstanding matters, exceptions and records reconciled.
|===

Each observation cites ledger events and uses `supporting`, `contrary` or
`insufficient` polarity. Scores cannot be inferred from outcome alone.
