= Assessment Rubric v0.1
:toc: left

== Scale

Each competency receives `not-observed`, `developing`, `threshold`, or
`strong`, with cited observations. Critical errors are separate and cannot be
averaged away. A result may be invalidated by ledger/rule-pack integrity
failure.

== Route classes

`competent-standard`::
Proportionate evidence gathering, explicit uncertainty, defensible selection,
timely protection and adequate record.

`competent-alternative`::
A different route or sequence that remains defensible on the evidence then
known and mitigates its distinct risks.

`minimally-acceptable`::
Threshold reasoning with limited depth but no critical omission.

`common-error`::
Weakly supported assumption, avoidable delay, incomplete comparison or poor
record that does not itself invalidate the case.

`critical-error`::
Knowing action without authority, fabrication/alteration of evidence, hiding a
material conflict, certificate/ledger tampering, or proceeding when the
selected route's essential prerequisite is known to be absent.

== Threshold

`threshold-demonstrated` requires threshold evidence in engagement,
information, procedure comparison, evidence use, uncertainty, timeliness,
ethical reasoning and record quality; no critical error; a valid compatible
pack; and final submission. Other competencies contribute feedback and may
raise the result to `completed-with-feedback` without inventing an award.

== Consequences

Asset value, business continuity, costs, evidence preservation, creditor and
employee effects, distribution abstraction, investigation reach and elapsed
time are reported separately. They may corroborate decisions but do not define
competence.
