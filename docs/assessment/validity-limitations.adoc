= Assessment Validity and Limitations

Phase A provides internal, scenario-bounded observations. It has no demonstrated
predictive validity for workplace performance, no external standard setting,
no awarding-body approval and no regulatory recognition. The provisional rule
pack is not comprehensive and has not received practitioner review.

Scores are conditional on scenario/version, disclosed evidence, rule pack,
rubric and ledger integrity. Accessibility barriers, group role allocation,
technical failure or compromised identity may require invalidation or a
separate accommodation record rather than a low competency inference.
