= Certification Boundary

The only Phase A award is a *Certificate of Completion and Assessed
Performance*. Its internal classes are `completed`, `completed-with-feedback`,
`threshold-demonstrated`, `threshold-not-yet-demonstrated`, and `invalidated`.

The certificate identifies learner/pseudonym, scenario/version, rule pack,
authority cut-off, rubric, completion time, competency results, critical-error
status, application version, ledger digest, certificate digest, signature
method, verification instructions and disclaimer.

It does not confer, imply or predict licensed practitioner status, JIEB success,
regulated competence, statutory authorisation, an academic award, supervised
experience or accredited CPD. Provisional or unreviewed rules cannot support a
professional-certification claim.
