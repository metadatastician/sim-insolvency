= Learning Model
:toc: left

== Construct

The assessed construct is defensible practice reasoning under uncertainty and
resource pressure. The simulator records what evidence the learner could
reasonably access at the time, not hindsight alone.

== Evidence of learning

Observations derive from requested and overlooked evidence, claims and their
support, explicit uncertainty, risk calibration, option comparison, reasons,
sequence, timing, resources, communications, obligation handling and later
belief correction.

== Feedback timing

Assessment mode provides process affordances and limitation notices but no
correctness feedback before final submission. Debrief links feedback to events,
disclosures, beliefs, rules and authorities.

== Route plurality

Rubrics define competent standard routes, competent alternatives, minimally
acceptable routes, common errors and critical errors. No rubric encodes one
secret click sequence. A route is judged using what was known at the decision
time and whether uncertainty and downside were handled proportionately.
