= Product Claims
:toc: left

== Permitted claims

* Fictional, source-attributed professional-learning simulator.
* Local-first case-based assessment of recorded reasoning and actions.
* Bounded educational models of CVL and Administration.
* Provisional England and Wales corporate-insolvency rule pack with declared
  review state and authority cut-off.
* Certificate of Completion and Assessed Performance, verifiable against the
  exported event-ledger digest.

== Prohibited claims

The project and its outputs must not claim legal advice, regulatory approval,
professional qualification, licensed-insolvency-practitioner status, JIEB
equivalence, statutory authority, guaranteed competence, comprehensive law, or
accredited CPD unless a named external body separately grants that status.

== Placement

The limitation appears in the README, Home and workspace shells, scenario
metadata, replay bundles, debrief, certificate and every exported simulation
document. Exported forms use a visible `SIMULATION — NOT FOR FILING` mark.
