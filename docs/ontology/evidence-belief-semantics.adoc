= Evidence and Belief Semantics
:toc: left

== Five layers

. *Scenario reality* is authored fictional truth and causal configuration.
. *Potential evidence* is material that might be requested, produced or lost.
. *Disclosed record* is the immutable history of material made available.
. *Learner belief state* contains learner-authored claims, hypotheses,
  uncertainties, risks and reasons.
. *Normative case state* contains active rules, obligations, permissions,
  prohibitions, deadlines and compliance risk.

No layer aliases another. Evidence is not automatically fact; a claim is not
true because it was recorded; normative state does not reveal hidden reality.

== Belief operations

`record-claim` requires text and may cite zero or more disclosures; uncited
claims are explicitly unsupported. `record-hypothesis` carries alternatives
and falsifiers. `record-uncertainty` carries impact and a possible resolution
request. `record-risk` carries likelihood, consequence, evidence and review
time. Later evidence may confirm, weaken, contradict or leave a belief
unresolved; history is appended, not overwritten.

== Disclosure

Evidence can be disclosed only after its authored availability time and an
accepted request/event, unless it is in the initial packet. Disclosed evidence
IDs are monotonically accumulated. Withholding is an event with a reason, not
an absence that the learner can distinguish from a missing implementation.

== Hidden-truth boundary

Assessment bundles delivered to the learner contain public manifests and sealed
references only. Reality files remain in the trusted local kernel/facilitator
process. IDs are random/opaque and filenames do not encode conclusions.
