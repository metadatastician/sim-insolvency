= Rule Review Policy

Two-person review is required for executable normative scoring: a
subject-matter practitioner and an editor responsible for source identity,
effective dates and faithful representation. Review records name the reviewed
pack digest. Any content change invalidates the review until renewed.

`UNREVIEWED` rules may not affect a professional-certification claim.
`PROVISIONAL` rules may drive clearly labelled formative observations.
Revocation stops authoritative scoring without deleting historical replay.
