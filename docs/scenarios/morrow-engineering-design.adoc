The company is a fictional small manufacturer under acute cash pressure.
Management information is incomplete. It has asset-finance obligations, HMRC
arrears, employee liabilities, disputed trade debt, two directors giving
different accounts, a potentially overdrawn director loan account, a recent
connected-party payment, a secured creditor considering action and an
interested purchaser of uncertain reliability. None of those learner-visible
ingredients asserts a legal conclusion.

== Shared pre-appointment phase

The learner must establish engagement scope/independence, obtain authority,
triage immediate cash and asset risks, request accounts/bank/security/employee/
tax/contract information, interview directors, record uncertainty, compare
procedures and document advice. Time and enquiry budget prevent exhaustive
investigation.

== Procedure comparison

Informal restructuring::
Potentially low cost and continuity-preserving, but conditional on credible
short-term funding, creditor forbearance, reliable information and governance.

CVA::
Genuinely considered where a viable underlying business and fundable proposal
can be evidenced. Bounded Phase A outcome is recommendation/referral because a
full CVA lifecycle is not implemented.

Administration::
Requires reasoned statutory-purpose and appointment-route analysis. It may
preserve business/assets long enough for a bounded sale/rescue decision, but
adds cost and control consequences. The branch covers control, notification,
proposals, creditor implications, sale/rescue analysis, reporting and an exit
that may include CVL.

CVL::
May be proportionate where rescue is unsupported and cessation/liquidation is
appropriate. The branch covers advice, decisions at an educational abstraction,
appointment, control, records/director enquiries, assets, claims,
investigation, reporting, distribution abstraction and closure.

Compulsory liquidation::
HMRC or another creditor can escalate. Delay can remove options or end the
bounded case in referral/terminal consequence.

Continued trading or cessation::
This is an action dimension, not a standalone formal procedure. The learner
must justify risk, authority, funding and preservation effects.

== Stable reality and variable consequences

Historical facts never change with the selected route. Outcomes vary through
elapsed time, deterioration, creditor action, purchaser confidence, director
co-operation, protected/lost evidence, communication and resource allocation.
All variability is seed- and command-deterministic.

== Workstreams

All thirteen required workstreams exist. The shared phase activates engagement,
financial analysis, advice and communications. Selection activates appointment
and route-specific work; estate protection, employees, creditors, directors,
investigations, funds/reporting and closure can overlap.

== Assessment hooks

The scenario rewards source-linked belief, explicit uncertainty, timely
secured-creditor/HMRC engagement, proportionate purchaser diligence, protection
of records/assets, comparison of rescue assumptions and an auditable decision.
It does not reward naming a supposed preference or breach without evidence and
authority.

== Content safety

All names and documents are fictional and visibly watermarked. Reality is kept
outside learner bundles. The authored design contains no proprietary training
question and no claim of current comprehensive law.
