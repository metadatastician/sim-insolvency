== Start here

Sim Insolvency is an evidence-driven, time-aware professional-learning
simulator. It is intended to help people practise how to gather information,
handle uncertainty, compare insolvency procedures, manage concurrent work and
make defensible decisions in a fictional corporate-insolvency case.

[IMPORTANT]
====
It is not legal advice, a law firm, an insolvency practice, live case-management
software, supervised experience, statutory authorisation, a professional
qualification, a JIEB result, or accredited CPD unless a competent external
body separately approves a clearly identified offering.
====

The programme is broader than this repository. It includes the simulator,
authored scenarios, authority maintenance, professional review, assessment
validity, group learning, deployment, client implementation, evaluation and
long-term operation. This wiki is its human navigation layer. Source-controlled
specifications and ADRs remain authoritative where they are more precise.

== Where the programme is now

The native Phase A vertical slice is executable and includes deterministic
replay, a bounded Morrow Engineering scenario, CVL and Administration paths,
assessment/debrief separation and a development certificate envelope. Several
intended estate integrations remain behind ports or test doubles. The initial
rule pack is provisional and not practitioner reviewed.
