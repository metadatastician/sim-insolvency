The only initial award label is *Certificate of Completion and Assessed
Performance*. It records learner or pseudonymous identity, scenario and rule
pack versions, authority cut-off, rubric and application versions, completion
time, competency results, critical-error status, ledger digest, certificate
digest, verification instructions and disclaimer.

Its internal result class may be `completed`, `completed-with-feedback`,
`threshold-demonstrated`, `threshold-not-yet-demonstrated` or `invalidated`.
These are application results, not regulated or academic awards. A valid digest
shows that the envelope has not changed since issue; it does not prove identity,
legal correctness, independent accreditation or professional competence beyond
the specified simulated assessment.
