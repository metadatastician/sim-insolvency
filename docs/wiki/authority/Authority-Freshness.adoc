Every normative assessment rule must identify jurisdiction, source, citation,
effective dates, interpretation note, pack version, review status and tests.
Each scenario declares an authority cut-off. Authoritative scoring must stop
when the requested pack is absent, incompatible or revoked, or when mandatory
sources lack the required review status.

Freshness is an operational process, not a timestamp alone: source adapters are
monitored, proposed changes are triaged, legal interpretation is reviewed,
affected scenarios and rubric observations are retested, and superseded packs
remain identifiable for deterministic replay. The initial England and Wales
corporate-insolvency pack is deliberately provisional and incomplete.
