[IMPORTANT]
====
Sim Insolvency is not a law firm, insolvency practice, live case-management
system, legal advice service, substitute for supervised experience, route to
statutory authorisation, professional qualification, JIEB examination result,
or accredited CPD unless a competent external body separately approves a
clearly delimited offering.
====

It does not accept live case data, make appointments, file statutory documents,
exercise office-holder powers, determine disputed law, or certify regulatory
competence. Simulation documents must remain visibly watermarked. Provisional
or unreviewed rules cannot support a professional-certification claim.
