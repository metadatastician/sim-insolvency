Sim Insolvency is a local-first, evidence-driven and time-aware practice
simulator. It combines an authored fictional reality, controlled evidence
disclosure, an append-only decision record, versioned authority packs,
concurrent workstreams, deterministic consequences and evidence-based
assessment. Phase A centres on Morrow Engineering Ltd and supports a common
advisory phase followed by bounded CVL or Administration lifecycles, while CVA,
informal restructuring and compulsory-liquidation risk remain genuine options
for analysis.

It is designed as a programme: software, scenarios, authority maintenance,
professional review, facilitation, evaluation, client implementation and
operational governance are all necessary to sustain a trustworthy product.
