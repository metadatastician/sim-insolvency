== Plain-language status

The programme has moved beyond conception and generic scaffolding. Its
foundation is specified and an executable technical vertical slice exists.
However, the requested architecture is not yet proven end to end, the scenario
and rubric have not received practitioner validation, and the authority pack is
provisional. The honest description is *pre-alpha with partial L2–L4 evidence*.

No gate is retrospectively marked closed merely because related files or tests
exist. G0–G2 need explicit decision records under this framework. G3 is the
current primary gate; G4 has meaningful implementation evidence but cannot be
declared complete until architecture, hidden-state, assessment and user-path
evidence are reviewed together.

== Capability state

Completed or demonstrable now:

* foundation and ontology specifications;
* append-only deterministic native kernel;
* Morrow Engineering scenario with bounded CVL and Administration branches;
* local evidence/notebook/procedure/assessment/debrief projections;
* golden-run, replay, ABI, scenario and certificate-tamper tests;
* offline single-learner reference mode;
* development standalone and container definitions.

In progress or partial:

* AffineScript principal-language migration;
* typed-wasm carrier verification;
* Gossamer-native shell;
* VeriSimDB persistence;
* bounded Burble group transport;
* Authority Watch relationship;
* professional rule/scenario/rubric review;
* documentation, accessibility and client implementation.

Blocked or externally dependent:

* usable Unified Hexadeca API package;
* production-complete Stapeln verification;
* accreditation or regulated recognition;
* practitioner availability and reviewed authority corpus.

== Top decisions awaiting resolution

. What evidence is sufficient to close G3 when estate components remain immature?
. Who may approve a released rule pack and Morrow scoring interpretation?
. What exact hidden-state packaging is acceptable for assessment deployments?
. Which certificate signing and verification governance is acceptable for pilots?
. What is the minimum accessible learner experience for alpha entry?

== Top programme risks

* hidden assessment truth leakage — machine-verified detection now exists
  (KEY-003 mitigated) but independent security review has not occurred;
* stale authority appearing current;
* insufficient professional-review capacity;
* AffineScript/typed-wasm gaps increasing boundary and test complexity;
* scenario scope overwhelming validation capacity;
* solo-maintainer overload and documentation drift.

== Pilot and release horizon

There is no active pilot and no committed release date. The planning horizon is
a bounded 18-month v1 pilot target, explicitly indicative and contingent on
professional validation, toolchain proof, authority governance, security and
accessibility evidence.

== Next three significant outcomes

. Adopt this programme framework and record G0–G2 decisions.
. Close or explicitly exception G3 with reproducible AffineScript, typed-wasm,
  Zig/Idris2, shell, storage and hidden-state evidence.
. Prepare Morrow Engineering, its authority mappings and rubric for structured
  practitioner and assessment review.
