== Accountability

The programme lead controls product scope, repository coordination and gate
preparation. That role cannot self-certify professional legal accuracy,
assessment validity, security independence or accreditation.

Named roles are required before the relevant gate:

* product/programme owner;
* technical architecture reviewer;
* insolvency practitioner or suitably qualified domain reviewer;
* legal-source/rule-pack reviewer;
* assessment-validity reviewer;
* security/privacy reviewer;
* accessibility reviewer;
* pilot facilitator and client sponsor where applicable.

Until appointed, those roles are `Unassigned`; their evidence cannot be
inferred from tests or prose.

== Decision and gate discipline

Gate closure requires a recorded decision with evidence, reviewers, residual
risks and date. A person with a material conflict should not be the only
reviewer. Provisional legal interpretations cannot become released scoring
rules without source and review evidence.

Scope changes use the v1 change-control rule. High work in progress, missing
review capacity or documentation drift activates EC-011 mitigations: reduce
scope, limit concurrent work, automate traceability checks and seek staged
external assistance.

== Programme records

Repository-controlled records include lifecycle/gates, decisions/ADRs, issue
hierarchy, event chains, key issues, traceability, page register, release
evidence and evaluation findings. GitHub projects are projections over these
records, not the only authoritative copy.

== Licensing and claims

Original code, documentation and fictional learning content use
PMPL-2.0-or-later. Third-party authority text and citations retain their own
rights. No proprietary course questions or protected training materials may be
copied. Commercial, accreditation, qualification or service claims require a
separate approved decision and evidence.
