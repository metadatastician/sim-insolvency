== Purpose

The extended programme exists to determine whether a bounded, evidence-driven
insolvency simulator can become a professionally defensible learning and
assessment product, and to build and operate that product if the evidence
supports it.

It joins several kinds of work which software delivery alone cannot settle:

* product and learner experience;
* fictional case design and evidence semantics;
* legal-source mapping and authority freshness;
* competency assessment and validity;
* professional review;
* privacy, security and accessibility;
* deployment, support, purchasing and institutional implementation;
* evaluation, maintenance and eventual retirement.

== What exists now

The repository contains foundation, ontology, event, evidence, rule-pack,
assessment, certificate, threat-model and architecture specifications. A native
Zig Phase A kernel exercises the Morrow Engineering scenario, both formal
branches, deterministic replay, golden runs, group-session test behaviour,
debrief and a development certificate envelope. Idris2 ABI definitions and an
AffineScript-facing contract are present.

The implementation does not yet prove that the entire intended product is
professionally valid or ready for institutional use. Gossamer, Burble,
VeriSimDB, typed-wasm, Hexadeca and Echidna relationships have varying maturity
and are capability-gated or represented by ports.

== Programme outcomes

A bounded v1 is successful only if:

. a learner can complete a coherent local-first assessment;
. the scenario and scoring have professional and assessment-validity evidence;
. authority identity and freshness are explicit;
. hidden truth and certificate evidence survive security review;
. the build and operation are reproducible;
. accessibility and privacy have been evaluated;
. facilitator and client implementation routes are supportable;
. released claims remain narrower than the evidence.
