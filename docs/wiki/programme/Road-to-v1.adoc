This 18-month view is an indicative planning target, not a release promise.
It assumes bounded scope, access to professional reviewers, sufficient
maintenance capacity and no material authority or toolchain event.
====

== Capability horizons

Now::
Adopt programme governance, formalise G0–G2 decisions, define v1, stabilise
documentation/traceability, close architecture gaps and prepare Morrow for
professional review.

Next::
Complete G3/G4 evidence, validate scenario/rubric/authority mappings, run
trusted alpha cohorts, resolve accessibility and hidden-state findings.

Later::
Conduct controlled institutional/professional pilot, complete operational and
client evidence, prepare and decide on a bounded release candidate.

Post-v1::
Additional cases, procedure lifecycles, jurisdictions, authoring tools, hosted
services and recognition partnerships.

Research horizon::
Generalised scenario assistance, stronger proof automation, broader authority
ingestion and assessment-reliability models.

== Reserve and contingency

The schedule requires explicit reserve for professional-review delay, authority
change, AffineScript/typed-wasm adapters, scenario reduction and accessibility
rework. Contingencies include narrowing group mode, retaining local adapters,
reducing procedural depth outside the two deep branches, delaying external
recognition and extending the pilot horizon. Professional validity, hidden-state
integrity and authority freshness are not acceptable schedule sacrifices.

== Forecast confidence
