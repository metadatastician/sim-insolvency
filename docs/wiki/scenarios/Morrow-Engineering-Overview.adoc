Morrow Engineering Ltd is a fictional small manufacturer under severe
cash-flow pressure. The disclosed case can include incomplete management
information, asset finance, HMRC and employee liabilities, disputed trade debt,
conflicting director accounts, a potentially overdrawn director loan account,
a recent connected-party payment, secured-creditor pressure and an uncertain
purchaser. These are matters to investigate, not learner-visible conclusions.

The same immutable scenario reality supports a shared pre-appointment advisory
phase and defensible CVL or Administration paths. CVA and informal
restructuring can be investigated and recommended or rejected at bounded depth.
Compulsory liquidation is a credible threat or consequence of delay. Events
vary with time, evidence, resource allocation and decisions—not by rewriting
history to fit the selected procedure.

The scenario is executable, but content scope, legal mappings, rubric validity
and hidden-truth isolation still require formal review.
