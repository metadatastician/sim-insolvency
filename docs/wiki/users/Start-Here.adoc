== What you will do

You will work through a fictional corporate-insolvency case. You inspect the
record available at the time, request evidence, record claims and uncertainty,
compare procedures, manage deadlines and resources, and explain consequential
decisions. The case can develop through a bounded CVL or Administration path.

Assessment considers the quality and timing of your reasoning, not merely the
financial outcome. A defensible alternative can score well; an apparently good
outcome reached through unsupported assumptions may not.

== Before starting

Use fictional or pseudonymous identity data. Do not enter live client, employee
or creditor information. In assessment mode, hints and hidden scenario reality
remain unavailable until submission. Submission is final for that run.

The result is a Certificate of Completion and Assessed Performance for the
identified simulation. It is not a qualification, licence, regulatory
authorisation, JIEB result, or evidence of supervised professional experience.
